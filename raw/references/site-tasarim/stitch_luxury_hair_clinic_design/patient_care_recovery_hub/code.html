The Golden Rules of Longevity

01.

Zero Contact

Strictly avoid touching or scratching the graft area for the first 10 days. Let the architecture settle undisturbed.

02.

Hydration Therapy

Apply the clinical spray every 30 minutes during waking hours to maintain optimal moisture levels for the follicles.

03.

UV Guard

Sunlight is the enemy of healing tissue. Wear our provided medical-grade headwear for 4 weeks post-op.

04.

Vascular Health

Avoid nicotine and heavy caffeine for 14 days to ensure maximum blood flow to the newly transplanted roots.

Post-Surgical
Precision

01

First 48 Hours

Absolute rest. Maintain head elevation at 45 degrees. Use only the provided saline spray for hydration. Avoid all physical exertion.

02

Week One

Begin gentle clinical washing. No direct shower pressure. Pat dry with sterile gauze only. Avoid wearing hats or helmets.

03

Two Weeks Onward

Normal hair washing can resume. Scabs should naturally fall away. Light cardiovascular exercise may be reintroduced gradually.

Recovery Inquiries

How long until I can wash my hair normally?

add
While gentle washing begins on Day 3, you can resume normal shower pressure and massage techniques after Day 14, once the grafts are securely integrated into the dermal layer.

When will I see the final aesthetic results?

add
The masterpiece takes time. Initial growth appears by Month 4, but full density and hair shaft maturation are typically reached at the 12-month mark.

When is it safe to resume heavy exercise?

add
Light cardio is acceptable after 14 days. However, heavy weightlifting and contact sports should be avoided for 4 weeks to prevent blood pressure spikes that could impact graft health.

Is the 'shedding phase' a cause for concern?

add
Not at all. The "Shock Loss" phase between Week 2 and Month 2 is a sign of a healthy follicular cycle. The hair falls out, but the living root remains to produce permanent, stronger hair.
